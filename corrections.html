Corrections Policy

The Halden Ledger is committed to correcting factual errors promptly and transparently.

Minor typographical, formatting or style changes may be made without notice when they do not alter the meaning of an article.

Corrections that materially affect the accuracy, context or interpretation of a published article will be noted on the article itself and recorded on this page.

When appropriate, a correction will identify what was wrong, provide the accurate information and note when the change was made.

To report a possible error, contact news@thehaldenledger.com.
