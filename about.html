About The Halden Ledger

The Halden Ledger is a fictional newspaper set in the near-future world of The Silenced Universe.

Its articles are written as if they were published in 2027 and beyond, covering politics, science, technology, culture, economics and the events surrounding the story.

Some articles connect directly to characters, organizations and events from The Silenced Universe. Others exist simply to show what everyday life looks like in a world that has begun to diverge from our own.

The goal is to make that world something readers can explore rather than something the novels have to stop and explain.

You may encounter people, companies and institutions that later appear in the books. You may also encounter developments that never become part of the main plot but help establish the world around it.

Is the reporting real?

No. The Halden Ledger is a work of fiction.

Real scientific, economic and social trends may be used as foundations for some articles, but the publication itself, its reporters, interviews, events and future developments should not be treated as real-world journalism.

The future dates are intentional.

The Ledger is designed to let readers briefly experience the world from the perspective of people living inside it—to read tomorrow’s news before tomorrow arrives.

The Silenced Universe

The Silenced Universe is a near-future science-fiction series beginning with A Decade to Midnight.

The Halden Ledger is one doorway into that universe.

You do not need to have read the books to explore the site, and the site is designed so that readers can discover the larger story at their own pace.
