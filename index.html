Science / The Moon

Themelion Enters a New Phase

Once dismissed as an expensive experiment, the lunar settlement is becoming something harder to ignore: permanent infrastructure beyond Earth.

By Naomi Valez
Themelion Base in the lunar south polar region. Halden Ledger file image.

Themelion was never supposed to look permanent this soon. Its earliest modules were designed around short-duration crews, narrow operational margins, and the assumption that almost everything important would still come from Earth. That assumption is beginning to change.

New construction, expanded logistics capacity, and a growing industrial footprint have turned the outpost into a test of whether human activity beyond Earth can become routine rather than exceptional.
