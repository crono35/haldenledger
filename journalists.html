The Halden Ledger

Our Journalists

Meet the reporters and editors behind the Ledger’s investigations, analysis and daily coverage.

Lena Kwon

Investigative Reporter

Lena reports on institutions whose public explanations do not match the available evidence. Her work combines patient source development, document analysis and a stubborn insistence that consequential claims be independently verified.

Martin Hale

Editor

Martin oversees the Ledger’s editorial work, challenging reporters to prove not only what happened, but what can responsibly be said about it. A veteran editor, he is known for rigorous sourcing, plain language and standing behind his newsroom when the reporting holds.

Elena Arden

Policy & Politics Correspondent

Elena covers national policy, political coalitions and the gap between public promises and governing power. Her reporting follows how decisions move from campaign language into budgets, agencies and law.

Naomi Valez

Science & Space Correspondent

Naomi reports on astronomy, planetary science and human activity beyond Earth. She specializes in translating complex research into clear consequences without flattening the uncertainty that remains.

Marcus Venn

Technology Correspondent

Marcus covers artificial intelligence, automation and the industrial systems reshaping how work gets done. He focuses on what emerging technologies can actually do—and who bears the consequences when deployment outruns oversight.

Owen Guillermo

Global Economy Correspondent

Owen follows trade, labor and the infrastructure connecting regional decisions to the global economy. His reporting examines the quiet dependencies beneath markets—and what happens when they begin to fail.

Theo Ryden

Features & Culture Writer

Theo writes interviews and features about the people living inside technological change. His work is less interested in polished reputations than in the habits, compromises and private convictions beneath them.
